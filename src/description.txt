[orange]DriveDogs: Radar[normal] - speed camera strobes and flashing speed traps

Every fining speed camera of the base game and all SCS map DLCs blinks a red police-style strobe, and every
police speed trap's parked patrol car flashes its lightbar, so you see them before they see you.
No sounds, no truck parts.

ATS has few of them: 5 speed cameras and 16 police speed traps on the whole map (ATS 1.61).

Want a "camera ahead" warning on a screen or LEDs? The DevDogs TruckSimulator SimHub plugin detects
speed cameras from the same camera database (separate download).
